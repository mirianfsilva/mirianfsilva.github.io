## About me
I'm an AI Engineer at IBM Research, collaborating globally on projects involving LLM evaluation and improvement, proprietary model quality (Granite Models), research prototyping, and software engineering in Python for generative AI. I have over 5 years of experience in AI/ML engineering, supported by data science expertise and software engineering. I hold a Master's degree in Computer Science from UFMG, Brazil, where my research focused on AI/ML fairness, bias mitigation, and evaluation practices. My academic journey also includes a B.Sc in Computational Mathematics from UFMG.

## Research Interest
In addition to my engineering background, I have research interests in areas such as fairness and bias in AI, within the broader context of AI ethics, AI safety, as well as AI for Good and interdisciplinary work at the intersection of AI and the social sciences. On the engineering side, my professional interests include AI/ML engineering, large-scale model evaluation, ML reliability and robustness, and applied AI engineering and prototyping. If you are working on these or related topics and are seeking research collaborations, feel free to reach out to me.
