A girl who like code, books, super heroes, math, computer science, harry potter and star wars. :)

Also Instructor and creator of Guided Projects on Coursera. Black in AI community member; a place for sharing ideas, fostering collaborations and discussing initiatives to increase the presence of Black people in the field of Artificial Intelligence. Also member of LatinX in AI and Women in Machine Learning Communities. Completely involved in actions that promote women in STEM and Founder and creator of Girls Support Girls Community in Belo Horizonte, Brazil.

Research topic of interest: Fairness and Bias in AI, Privacy, Explicability, Ethics & Trustworthy AI, Math Applied and Computational Biology applications.
