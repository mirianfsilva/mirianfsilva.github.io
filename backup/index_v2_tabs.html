About.

I'm an AI Engineer at IBM Research, collaborating globally on LLM evaluation and improvement, proprietary model quality (Granite models), research prototyping, and software engineering in Python for generative AI.

I have 5+ years of experience in AI/ML engineering, supported by a background in data science and software engineering. I hold a Master's degree in Computer Science from UFMG, where my research focused on AI/ML fairness, bias mitigation, and evaluation practices. My academic journey also includes a B.Sc in Computational Mathematics from the same university.

Research interests.

On the research side, I work at the intersection of AI fairness, AI safety, and the social sciences — questions like how bias enters ML pipelines, how to mitigate it in practice, and how AI systems land in real-world social contexts. On the engineering side, I focus on large-scale model evaluation, ML reliability and robustness, and applied AI prototyping.

  • Fairness & bias mitigation
  • AI safety & AI for good
  • LLM evaluation at scale
  • Model routing & quality
  • ML reliability & robustness
  • AI ↔ social sciences

If you're working on these or related topics and want to collaborate, please reach out.
